Subscribe, Donate, and custom buttons that stay in the corner of your public pages, with a QR code and a newsletter signup page.

== Description ==

* **Subscribe** opens a newsletter signup page for the BosqueGrande group you choose. Use the built-in page (default address /bg-newsletter-signup/) or any page of your own with a [bg_subscribe] form. Signups use BosqueGrande's double opt-in: people are subscribed only after they confirm by email.
* **Donate** opens your BosqueGrande giving page.
* **Custom link** points anywhere you like, such as a volunteer page.
* **QR code** for the first button on large screens, so people reading on a computer can open it on their phone. It is made on your own site; no outside service sees the address. Phones do not show it.

The buttons follow BosqueGrande's main color (or your own), choose black or white text for contrast, and stay off the signup, giving, registration, survey, My Profile, and organizer app pages. Labels and the signup page text are edited in BosqueGrande > Wording & Styling.

== Installation ==

1. Install and set up BosqueGrande 5.0 or later.
2. Upload this plugin's ZIP from Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin and activate it.
3. Choose your buttons under BosqueGrande > Floating Actions.
